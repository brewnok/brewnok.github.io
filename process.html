Our Process

A simple, transparent workflow to bring your vision to reality. No surprises, no hidden costs.

Step by Step

How we work with you

1

Tell Us Your Needs

Share your requirements and goals. We listen, ask the right questions, and provide a customized quote tailored to your business.

Typical turnaround for initial quote is 2–3 business days. We'll schedule a free 30-minute discovery call to understand your challenges.

2

Scope & Timeline

Detailed requirements gathering, project scope definition, milestones, and deadlines. You sign off on a clear plan before we write a single line of code.

We use Jira/Linear for project tracking and share access with you. Every milestone has a clear deliverable and acceptance criteria.

3

First Demo

We present an initial build and prototype to gather your direct feedback. No surprises — you see exactly what we're building.

Demos are typically scheduled within 2–4 weeks depending on project complexity. We record every demo for your reference.

4

Refinement & Final Demo

Based on your feedback, we polish, optimize, and present the final demonstration for sign-off. This is where the product comes together.

We iterate until you're 100% satisfied. No extra charges for reasonable revisions. Your approval gates every release.

5

Delivery & Support

The final build is delivered, fully tested, launched, and supported. We set up monitoring, hand over documentation, and stay available.

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support at no additional cost. Extended support plans are available.

Why It Works

Built on three pillars

Transparency

You see everything. No black boxes, no hidden timelines. Full access to project tracking, code repos, and real-time progress.

Speed

Agile sprints with weekly check-ins. We ship fast without cutting corners. Continuous delivery from day one.

Partnership

We don't disappear after launch. Long-term support is built into our DNA. Your success is our only KPI.
