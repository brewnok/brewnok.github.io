About Brewnok

We are engineers, problem-solvers, and automation obsessives based in Agartala, Tripura, India.

Our Story


We are a technology solutions company focused on helping businesses build, scale, and operate reliable digital systems. From web and software development to IT infrastructure, cloud solutions, Site Reliability Engineering, observability, and automation, we bring together modern engineering practices to create solutions that are secure, scalable, efficient, and built for long-term growth.

We don't just keep systems running, we make them better. We design technology that performs reliably, scales effortlessly, provides clear visibility into what's happening, and automates repetitive operations wherever possible. Our goal is to reduce complexity, improve performance, and help businesses move faster with technology they can depend on.
