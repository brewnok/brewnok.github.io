Appointment Management System (AMS)

Schedmate

Streamlined, automated client scheduling engineered for zero friction.

Deploy Schedmate

Schedmate is a comprehensive Appointment Management System (AMS) developed by Brewnok, designed to optimize and simplify the booking lifecycle for businesses where appointments are critical. By offering an intuitive client booking UI and a robust administrative control center, Schedmate prevents scheduling conflicts, minimizes no-shows, and enhances customer satisfaction.

Why Choose Schedmate?

📅 Efficient Management

Clients can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments online 24/7 in real time, eliminating administrative phone tag and freeing staff time.

⚡ Zero Double Bookings

Automated conflict detection prevents overlapping time slots and instantly synchronizes calendar capacity across all staff members.

🔔 Real-Time Notifications

Automated SMS and email reminders notify clients and admins, substantially cutting no-show rates and protecting revenue.

📊 Data Insights & Export

Access actionable metrics on appointment volume, peak hours, and customer trends, with one-click Excel data export for any date range.

📱 Device Responsive

A fast, mobile-friendly interface ensures effortless booking on smartphones, tablets, and desktops for both clients and staff.

📈 Scalable for Growth

Seamlessly scale from a single practitioner to multi-branch, multi-staff operations with customizable permissions and service tiers.

Engineered on the Modern MERN Stack

M

MongoDB

High-throughput document database using flexible BSON schemas. Supports horizontal sharding, compound indexing for instant slot lookup, and replication for 99.99% data availability.

E

Express.js

Lightweight Node.js framework delivering fast, non-blocking REST APIs. Efficiently handles high-concurrency booking requests with robust middleware and security filters.

R

React.js

Declarative, component-driven frontend with Virtual DOM rendering. Delivers frictionless client booking flows, real-time calendar re-renders, and interactive state management.

N

Node.js

Event-driven, asynchronous V8 JavaScript runtime. Enables seamless concurrent processing of notification queues, automated reminders, and background scheduler jobs.

Core Features at a Glance

Client-Facing Booking Flow

Clean, frictionless user interface for customers to pick a service, select an available date from an interactive calendar, choose an open time slot, provide contact info, and receive immediate booking confirmation.

Admin Operations Dashboard

Centralized view for owners and managers. Review pending requests, approve appointments, adjust time slots on the fly with the inline edit tool, and view daily/weekly appointment manifests.

Excel Data Export

Export appointment manifests by date or custom time periods directly into Excel format for bookkeeping, customer relationship records, and staff scheduling.

WhatsApp & Email Notification Engines

Instant automated transactional messages sent upon booking request and admin approval, keeping both the business and client fully aligned without manual communication.

Hospitality & Property Solution

ManagerJi

Ultra-fast, offline-capable hotel management engineered for one-go learning.

Deploy ManagerJi

ManagerJi is a simple yet powerful end-to-end hotel management solution designed to streamline front-desk operations, room allocations, in-room service billing, and guest checkouts. Unlike bloated legacy hotel software, ManagerJi is designed for "one-go learning" — staff can navigate and run the entire property confidently after completing a single operation.

How ManagerJi Outperforms Traditional Hotel Software

⚡ True Offline-First Architecture

Unlike 90% of generic apps requiring a constant internet link, ManagerJi runs uninterrupted even during total internet blackouts using local storage. Internet is only required for remote monitoring sync and email dispatches.

🎯 Zero-Bloat, 1-Go Learning

Front-desk staff do not require multi-day training seminars. The visual color-coded layout (Green = Available, Red = Occupied) allows anyone to check in guests within 30 seconds.

💻 Ultra-Light Browser Footprint

Operates directly in modern web browsers without heavy desktop installations, keeping older front-desk computers running lightning-fast without system lag.

Comprehensive Operational Walkthrough

1. Landing Screen & Room Matrix

Displays total days left before license renewal, live counters of Available Rooms (Green) and Occupied Rooms (Red), and clean tab switches between Floor 1, Floor 2, and Floor 3 for instant visibility.

2. Dynamic Guest Check-In

Click any green block to open the Check-In modal. Enter Guest Name, Phone, Aadhaar Card Number, and Per-Day Room Price. Increasing the "Number of Adults" dynamically spawns additional input fields for secondary guests.

3. In-Room Service Management

Clicking an occupied red room opens a slide-in right drawer. Front desk can order restaurant items, bottled water, or laundry services using preset prices and interactive quantity knobs. Total charges recalculate instantly.

4. Itemized Checkout & Auto-Backup

One-click checkout displays full guest details, timestamped room service line items, calculated stay duration, and room charges. Confirming checkout prints/exports itemized bills and triggers automatic Excel backups.

Customization & Security Guarantees

Custom Floor & Room Config

Easily adapt room counts, luxury suite tiers, and floor numbers to match your property's exact architectural layout.

Flexible Service Catalog

Configure restaurants, bars, laundry, spa, or minibar service menus with customized price lists stored in the backend.

Automated Secure Backups

Guest archives, financial tallies, and occupancy logs are securely backed up in Excel format for audit readiness and tax compliance.

Fitness & Health Club Management

Hupwiz

End-to-end gym operations, membership tracking, and automated renewals.

Deploy Hupwiz

Hupwiz is a comprehensive gym and fitness center management platform engineered by Brewnok. It empowers gym owners to transition seamlessly from fragile paper-based registers to a high-performance digital hub that handles member onboarding, recurring payment collection, personal trainer assignments, and revenue analytics.

The 4 Core Operational Modules

1. Analytics & Executive Dashboard

Real-time visual dashboard showcasing Active Members, Pending Payments, New Registrations, Monthly Revenue, and Membership Plan Distribution. Includes an "Export All Data" feature to generate Excel reports across any selected date range.

2. Streamlined Member Registration

Intuitive form collecting member details: Full Name, Phone, Body Weight (kg), Gender, Personal Trainer requirement (Yes/No with Trainer Fee), Admission Fee, Plan Type (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual), and Payment Mode (Cash/Online). Auto-generates a unique Member Registration ID (e.g., 2025003T).

3. Recurring Payments & Renewals Engine

Automatically surfaces members with dues in the current calendar month. Tracks initial payment under "Registration" and subsequent cycles under "Renewal". Allows managers to mark dues as "Paid" or "Unpaid", search by Registration ID, and export payment sheets.

4. Comprehensive Member Directory

Searchable membership repository with filters for personal trainers, plan tiers, and status. Expand any profile to review join date, renewal due dates, trainer assignments, and historical fee payments, or edit member profiles on demand.

Scalable Cloud Stack

Built on Brewnok's enterprise MERN architecture (MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js), ensuring rapid data queries, instant member check-ins, and rock-solid uptime during peak morning and evening workout rushes.

Simple 4-Step Gym Onboarding: To configure Hupwiz for your fitness center, provide: (1) Gym logo and legal name, (2) Preferred admin login credentials, (3) Custom registration fields (e.g. locker numbers, emergency contacts), and (4) Your membership tiers and pricing matrix. Brewnok handles complete deployment and data provisioning.
Residential & Apartment Operations

BuildingBugs

Unified resident ticketing and property maintenance management system.

Deploy BuildingBugs

BuildingBugs is an end-to-end ticketing and facility maintenance platform developed for residential complexes, gated societies, and modern apartment towers. It bridges the communication gap between flat owners/tenants and building management, allowing residents to log repair issues or request daily necessities (newspapers, water, milk) while giving property managers complete operational oversight.

Operational Flow & Visual Monitoring

Unified Dual-Portal Login

A single branded landing page for both residents and facility managers. Residents log in as Customer to access their flat portal; management toggles to Admin Login with secured credentials.

Color-Coded Tower & Flat Overview

Management dashboard renders an interactive map of all towers. Towers with open tickets automatically turn Red, while issue-free towers stay Green. Admins can click any tower to inspect individual flat statuses.

User & Flat Credential Management

Admins assign and provision accounts to flats through the User Management tab. When tenants vacate or move, accounts can be decommissioned or reassigned with a single click, keeping building access secure.

Frictionless Ticket Submission

Residents submit tickets by selecting issue category (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, Daily Supplies), adding descriptions (e.g. "Kitchen exhauster is not working"), and clicking Submit. Instant WhatsApp/email notifications alert management.

Technician Dispatch & Ticket Closure

Admins filter flats marked in red, review the logged problem, and assign the appropriate technician or electrician. Once repairs are completed and verified with the resident, the ticket is formally closed with an audit log timestamp.

Future Enhancement Scope

1. In-App Tower & Flat Editing

Admins will be empowered to dynamically add, rename, or retire new towers and flats directly within the administrative settings.

2. Automated Maintenance Analytics

Quarterly and monthly reports detailing recurring issue types, mean resolution times, and recurring facility bottlenecks for long-term planning.

3. Self-Registration & KYC Module

Automated tenant verification and self-onboarding portal, requiring a single manual approval click from the building administrator.

4. Dedicated Mobile Applications

Native iOS and Android apps with push notifications for real-time ticket tracking, guard gate notifications, and amenity booking.
